# Purpose

Onboarding is the process of bringing a new volunteer onto the clinic team and getting them set up in the volunteer management system. It covers everything from receiving an application to creating the volunteer's account and assigning their first shifts.

# Pages

- Admin dashboard Pipeline tab: review applications and move them through the interview and onboarding steps.
- Admin dashboard Add Volunteer tab: create a new volunteer's account and profile.
- Admin dashboard Volunteers tab: view the roster, edit volunteer details, and manage credentials.
- Admin dashboard Scheduling tab: assign shifts once a volunteer is onboarded.

# Workflows

1. Receive a volunteer application. It appears on the Pipeline tab.
2. Review the application and move it to the interview stage once you have spoken with the applicant.
3. After a successful interview, move the applicant to the onboarding step.
4. Complete the onboarding wizard: collect affiliation, position, availability, documents, and the required checklist items.
5. Create the volunteer's account through the Add Volunteer tab. The new volunteer joins the team and is added to the waitlist.
6. Assign the volunteer's first recurring shifts on the Scheduling tab.
7. Welcome the volunteer and show them how to log in, view their schedule, and clock in and out.

# Business rules

- Non-missionary volunteers need a background check, ID, and immunization documents before their account can be created.
- Missionaries have relaxed document requirements.
- A volunteer cannot be assigned shifts until their account is created and they are active on the roster.

# User roles

- Admin: reviews applications, completes onboarding, creates accounts, and assigns shifts.
- Volunteer: once onboarded, logs in to view their schedule and clock in and out.

# Frequently asked questions

Q: How do I add a new volunteer?
A: Review their application on the Pipeline tab, complete the onboarding steps, then create their account on the Add Volunteer tab.

Q: What documents are required?
A: Non-missionary volunteers need a background check, ID, and immunization records. Missionaries have fewer required documents.

Q: When can a new volunteer start working shifts?
A: After their account is created on the Add Volunteer tab and they appear as active on the Volunteers tab.
